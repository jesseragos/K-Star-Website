Our Story

Activate Your
Potential

Train authentic Martial Arts & Contortion in Las Vegas

K-Star Training Academy was founded by Philip Sahagun and Ulziibayar Chimed, two former stars of the Cirque Du Soleil stage dedicated to sharing Authentic Martial Arts and Mongolian Contortion with the people of Las Vegas.

We have a team of qualified coaches and passionate teachers, whose mission is to offer high-level courses in a stimulating environment for the entire family. Each of our programs allows for specialization, and is available to people of all ages and skill sets. So whether you are looking for a new life goal or want to build up a future champion, we are here to help you along the way.
