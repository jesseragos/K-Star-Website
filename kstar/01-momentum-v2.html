Our story

From the Cirque stage to your family's studio.

High-level coaching in a stimulating environment — for the whole family.

Founded by Philip Sahagun and Vitalbayar Chimed — two former stars of the Cirque du Soleil stage — K-Star is dedicated to sharing authentic martial arts and Mongolian contortion with the people of Las Vegas. Our team of qualified coaches and passionate teachers offers high-level courses for every age and ability. Whether you're chasing a new life goal or building a future champion, we're here to help you along the way.
